Fig. 4 Correlations between raw auditory brainstem response (ABR) features and synapse count per inner hair cell (IHC) in Cohort A (Liberman). Each panel shows the relationship between one raw, untransformed ABR feature and synapse count (synapses per IHC) at the animal × frequency stratum, using the ABR measurement at the SPL level nearest to 80 dB. Panels are labeled A1–A10 and arranged as follows: A1 Amplitude; A2 Peak-to-trough latency; A3 Wave I slope; A4 Total variance; A5 Peak I curvature (early); A6 Peak I curvature (central); A7 Peak I curvature (late); A8 Trough I curvature (early); A9 Trough I curvature (central); A10 Trough I curvature (late). For all plots, symbols indicate individual animal × frequency strata and color indicates true noise exposure group (blue, low noise exposure; orange, high noise exposure). Spearman rank correlation coefficients (ρ) with approximate 95% confidence intervals are shown at the top right of each panel (format: *value (lower, upper); asterisk if p < 0.005, Bonferroni-corrected for 10 feature comparisons per figure). When significant, a single solid regression line is shown as a visual aid.
