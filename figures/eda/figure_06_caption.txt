Fig. 6 SHAP summary for the Stage-1 random forest noise classifier. Each panel shows SHAP values for ten aggregated ABR feature types (mean across SPL levels within type) at the animal × frequency stratum; frequency is omitted. Dots are colored by true noise exposure group (blue, low noise exposure; orange, high noise exposure). The model was trained on the Train split; SHAP values are computed on Validate and Test wide rows. SHAP values are on the probability scale for class-1 (noise-exposed). Features follow the same order as Fig. 4 (amplitude at top, reading downward). Panel A, Cohort A (Liberman); panel B, Cohort B (Brad).
